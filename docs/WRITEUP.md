# ร้านเรา · AI Store Manager

**Problem.** Owners of collectible shops sell the same scarce items online and over the counter. An advertisement, an online promise or a packing delay can change the viability of decisions elsewhere in the shop.

**Solution.** ร้านเรา lets the owner open a product in a 3D shop, evaluate advertising against stock, supplies, labor and cash, and ask an AI manager what to do. The manager uses scoped tools, requests a decision or checks a covering policy, then reads the result from the simulated destination. A separate rehearsal lets the owner test a campaign against a copy of the shop before saving a preparation plan.

**Build direction.** Autonomous & Adaptive AI is the primary direction. The owner can increase authority after verified outcomes and the service lowers it when a result cannot be verified or an automatic change is reversed. Deep Domain AI appears in the inspectable store calculations. A ฿300 ROBOT ad needs two incremental sales in the seeded shop. In the default 10.10 rehearsal the shop faces 395 units of packing work against daily capacity of 120.

**Use of Codex.** Codex converted the reviewed specification into a deterministic simulator, tested cash and stock formulas against order records, implemented the five-tool manager and Thai interface, and exercised approvals, shared resets, mobile layouts and deployment. The verification record identifies fixtures, actual browser actions and the status of live model calls.
