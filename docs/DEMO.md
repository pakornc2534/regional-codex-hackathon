# Six-minute demo

Start from **เริ่มเดโม**. The coach starts a fresh shared run. It observes actual changes and also lets the viewer advance manually. The date in the shop is simulated.

| Time | Click | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:40 | Open **หน้าร้านของคุณ**. Select ROBOT on the central pedestal, or use the selected-product button. | The product card shows 420 physical, 13 reserved and 407 available. Rotate the gallery or switch to 2D. S1, S18. |
| 0:40–1:40 | Click **ยิงแอด** then **ประเมินแผนนี้** with the one-day defaults. | Two additional sales recover ฿300. Baseline demand is 17, required total is 19. Packing can handle 25 and contribution rises ฿58.70. Click **บันทึกแผน**. S6, S8. |
| 1:40–2:25 | In **แชทลูกค้า** select SONIC, enable **เปิดข้อความรับเรื่อง**, and send a fabricated stock inquiry using **จำลองลูกค้าส่งข้อความ**. | One acknowledgement is sent for the waiting episode. Use **วิเคราะห์เคสนี้** in the SONIC reply case if no job is already running. S9. |
| 2:25–3:10 | Read the manager proposal, choose **ส่งข้อความนี้**, then **เปิดข้อความที่ส่ง**. | The chosen customer receives the approved text. The receipt links to the delivered message. The separate prompt controls future authority. S10, S14, S16. |
| 3:10–3:55 | In **สต็อกกลาง** search SONIC, open the product, expand the sales controls and sell one at POS. | Physical 1, reserved 1, available 0. The channel confirms zero. An existing unapproved reply becomes stale. An active SONIC ad with no available stock costs an assumed ฿5/hour or ฿15 over three hours. S2, S17. |
| 3:55–4:55 | Open **ซ้อมแคมเปญใหญ่**, select 10.10 and evaluate ×5. Choose **เตรียมวัสดุและเพิ่มกำลังแพ็ก**. | Inspect the first break, due dates, missed orders, before/after comparison and replenishment reminders. The initial run's results are available in verification. S20. |
| 4:55–5:35 | Open **บรีฟประจำวัน**, click **เดินเวลา 10 นาที**, then return to **ภาพรวมร้าน**. | One 07:00 brief summarizes waiting and autonomous work. The scorecard traces its numbers to receipts. S16, S19. |
| 5:35–6:00 | Show the linked verification log and the Node branch's milestone history. | Codex generated and reconciled the shop, implemented the guardrails and tested them through the UI and the deployed service. |

If the model is disconnected, show the reported status. The ROBOT plan, POS stockout, simulated fulfillment, rehearsal and brief still run. Skip the two model-dependent steps and describe the fixture evidence separately.
